CampusCommute – Project Summary: 

Project Objectives:  

Campus Commute is an exclusive campus ride sharing app that helps students and staff travel to and from university campuses in a safe and affordable way. The objectives we want to achieve with this app is firstly we want to simplify commutes by providing an app that's specifically for students that can request or offer rides directly within their university community. Secondly, this app will allow for an increased accessibility where it supports reliable and real-time campus travel through carpooling or reservations at an affordable price compared to other external apps. Thirdly, this app will promote sustainability by reducing traffic, single occupancy car use and encouraging students to take shared rides to their university campus. Lastly, this app will enhance safety by using university login verification, driver ratings to maintain a secure environment for users. 

Key Users: 

The key users that will be using this app are 

Students - the main target audience for this app, who need really affordable rides to and from campus. 

University Faculty and Staff - These are users that need reliable transportation between different campus locations. 

Campus Drivers - These are verified users that post available seats or routes for others to join. 
 

Key Scenarios: 

Ride Posting - A student driver heading to campus posts a ride with available seats. Other students nearby can request to join, confirm a pickup point and then split the costs with the other riders. 

Late Night Ride Back -  There is a student that just finished a 10 pm exam and uses the app to request a ride back home from verified campus drivers only, with live tracking for security. 

Travelling to other Campus locations - A faculty member traveling to another campus location ,  checks the app schedule to find available staff carpools departing soon.


Design Principles: 


Safety and Verification;  Limit the app to users that have verified university email accounts and in app ratings for drivers.  

Simplicity: The app is simple to use where users can post a ride, request a seat or view available routes

Sustainability: This app encourages eco-friendly practices by prioritizing shared rides and reducing carbon footprint. 

University Community Building: Strengthen the campus network by connecting students , promoting trust within the campus community.
