Competition Analysis:

Objective:  
Identify existing products that help users share rides, and explain how CampusCommute is different and better for York University students.


Existing Products:
Below are current tools or platforms that partially solve the commuting problem.

1. Uber / Lyft  
   - URL: https://www.uber.com, https://www.lyft.com  
   - What they do: On-demand paid rides with professional or part-time drivers.  
   - Limitations: Expensive for daily campus commuting; designed for point-to-point one-time trips, not recurring carpools; not restricted to students.

2. Poparide 
   - URL: https://www.poparide.com  
   - What they do: Long-distance carpool platform connecting drivers and passengers between cities.  
   - Limitations: Not focused on short, repetitive commutes like daily trips to campus; verification system is general-public, not school-specific.

3. Posts on Reddit / Discord servers
   - URL:  https://www.reddit.com/r/yorku, https://discord.gg/yorku 
   - What they do: Informal posts where students manually arrange rides.  
   - Limitations: Unorganized, no booking or schedule management, trust and safety issues, posts get buried easily.



How CampusCommute Is Different:
- York-exclusive access: Login using YorkU email ensures all users are verified students, increasing trust and safety.  
-Recurring ride scheduling: Drivers can post repeating daily or weekly rides to save time.  
- Built-in booking and reminders: Riders can book seats directly through the app, receive confirmations and reminders.  
- Focus on community: Emphasizes connections among classmates and eco-friendly commuting, not profit.
-Affordable travel: Sharing rides helps students cut commuting costs compared to Uber or gas expenses when driving alone. 








Specific Users and Scenarios:
 Users:
  - Commuter students who drive daily from cities like Mississauga, Brampton, or Markham.  
  - Non-drivers who need affordable, reliable rides aligned with class times.

Scenarios:
  - Alice drives from Milton to York each morning, posts an open ride for classmates.  
  - Bob finishes late class, searches for a ride back to Scarborough.  



Why Competitors Don’t Target These Users:
- Large platforms (Uber, Poparide) are designed for mass markets, not specific universities.  
- Building campus-specific verification and trust systems requires custom authentication and smaller-scale infrastructure, which big players skip because the audience is limited.  
- Social media groups lack structure and safety measures.


What It Would Take for Competitors to Match Us:
- Add student-email-based authentication.  
- Introduce short-distance, recurring-trip features.  
- Implement seat booking and in-app scheduling, all of which we design specifically for York’s community.
